RPE AND VELOCITY RELATIONSHIPS FOR THE BACK SQUAT, BENCH PRESS, AND DEADLIFT IN POWERLIFTERS
Helms, Storey, Cross, Brown, Lenetsky, Ramsay, Dillen, Zourdos (2017)
Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research 31(2): 292–297
===============================================================================================


STUDY OVERVIEW
--------------
N = 15 powerlifters (12 male, 3 female)
Protocol: worked up to 1RM on squat, bench, deadlift in one session
Velocity recorded at 80% estimated 1RM and above using GymAware
RPE scale: Zourdos RIR-based (10 = max, 9 = 1 rep in reserve, etc.)

RPE SCALE (Zourdos RIR-Based — as used in this study)
-------------------------------------------------------

10     Maximal effort — no reps remaining

8.5    1–2 reps in reserve

(and so on)


KEY PRACTICAL NOTES
-------------------
- ACV at 1RM differs significantly between lifts:
    Squat (0.23) > Deadlift (0.14) > Bench press (0.10)
  Do NOT use the same velocity → RPE table across lifts.

- The regression equations explain 81–85% of the variance in %1RM
  from velocity, but with ±5% individual error — individual
  load-velocity profiling gives more precise prescriptions.

- Data was collected only at ≥80% 1RM. Extrapolating the regression
  equations to lighter loads is not validated by this study.

- RPE converged to ~9.6–9.7 at 1RM across all three lifts, confirming
  the RIR-based RPE scale is consistent across movement patterns.
